Pushsky privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026

Pushsky is made by Fava LLC. It's an app for posting your photos to Bluesky and Mastodon. We don't run any servers for it, we don't collect your data, and there are no ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your phone

  • Drafts, including the original photos, which can contain where they were taken. They're kept out of iCloud and device backups.
  • Your posting history: the text and alt text of posts you made with Pushsky, smaller copies of their photos (without location or camera details), and which accounts each went to, with links. History is included in your iCloud or device backups.
  • Your sign-ins to Bluesky and Mastodon, kept in the iOS Keychain on this device only.

None of this is sent to us.

What leaves your phone

  • When you post, your photos, caption and alt text go to the Bluesky and Mastodon accounts you choose. The photo files are re-saved without any location or camera data first. Unless you remove them, your caption includes the camera, lens, settings and date read from your photos, plus a place name if you add one. What you post is public and is handled under each service's own privacy policy.
  • When you tap "Add location", the location stored in your photos is sent to Apple's map service to find place names. Only a place name you choose to add is ever posted, never the location itself.
  • Signing in happens on your Bluesky server's or Mastodon server's own sign-in page, so Pushsky never sees your password. To find that page, Pushsky looks up your handle or server. When you open the app, it checks your sign-ins with those services, and when you sign out, it tells them to end the sign-in.
  • Account suggestions: what you type in the Bluesky handle box, or after "@" in a caption, is sent to Bluesky's public search or to your Mastodon server to suggest accounts. Your Mastodon server may look up accounts on other servers to do this.
  • Public information: Pushsky reads public information from Bluesky and its image servers, such as profile pictures, who you follow and who follows you (for @mention suggestions), and the likes, reposts, replies and quotes on your posts. For Mastodon, these come from your own server and the servers it uses for images.

Removing your data

Delete a draft, or remove a post from History, in the app. Sign out of an account to remove its sign-in from your phone; iOS can keep Keychain items after an app is deleted, so signing out first is the sure way. Deleting the app removes everything else it keeps. Posts you've made are removed on Bluesky or Mastodon themselves.

Children

Pushsky isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
